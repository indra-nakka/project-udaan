# Udaan — Story & Narrative Design

_Session 066. The narrative spine for the kids' (5–10) build-and-play drone game. Grounded in research (Indian cities, FPV racing, respectful disability representation). Principle throughout: **show, don't tell; assume a smart player** — the world supplies the obstacles, the hero supplies the skill._

## Logline

An underdog girl who dreams of flying gets her first drone for her birthday and, city by city across India, learns to build and fly — rising from her Hyderabad rooftop to become the country's best FPV racer. She's a wheelchair user; the sky is where she's free, and she earns every win herself.

## Protagonist — the underdog (default name: **Ira**, player-selectable)

**The player names the hero, Prof-Oak style** — _"Erm… what's your name again? 😄"_ — **default `Ira`**. The *game* is called **Udaan** ("to soar"); the hero's name is **separate and different** from the title. A bright, funny, stubborn kid with a rooftop full of dreams. She is a **paraplegic wheelchair user** — and that is a matter-of-fact part of a full life, never the plot. She self-propels a light everyday chair, does her own transfers, fixes her own drone, and (later) races from a dedicated **sport chair** with racing stripes. The drone is her wings.

**Representation guardrails (non-negotiable — hire a disabled woman wheelchair-user to review):**
- **DO:** give her full **agency** (she wins by her own skill and choices); full **personhood** (humor, flaws, a temper, a rival she bickers with, things she's bad at unrelated to disability); **authentic detail** (self-propels, pops curbs, transfers into the race seat, owns a sport chair; nobody pushes or grabs her "to help"); **disabled joy & community** (disabled friends, celebratory wheelies, accommodations treated as ordinary — it's Tuesday, not a Very Special Episode).
- **AVOID:** **"inspiration porn"** (she is not here to make abled players feel good); **cure / "walks again" endings** — she never gets "fixed," the win is the trophy + crew + a more accessible world; **pity/tragedy/burden** framing; **the "supercrip"** ("amazing *because* disabled"); disability as her whole personality or as villain-coding. She beats the **rivals and the clock**, never her own body.
- **Social model in the design:** when something blocks her, make the *venue* the obstacle (an out-of-reach booth, no ramp) and let her and her friends **fix the environment** — never her body.

## Tone & principles

- **Show, don't tell.** Establish longing, skill, and growth through *what we see her do*, not narration. Assume a smart player — let them infer, discover, and miss things. That gap **drives replayability** (kids re-fly to find what they skipped).
- **Warm, playful, kind, safe.** No death, no cruelty; bullies are beaten with skill and turned into friends. Bright, hopeful, Ghibli-warm.
- **Earned, not gifted.** Every advance is hers — she builds it, flies it, learns it.

## Opening (the dream → the gift)

1. **Dream flight.** We open *inside a dream*: a kid in pyjamas soaring through soft, surreal dreamscapes, weaving through impossible, wondrous shapes — pure joy of flight, no UI, no rules. (Doubles as a gentle no-fail flight tutorial.)
2. **"…time to wake up."** Mum's voice pulls her down; the dream dissolves.
3. **Reality, matter-of-factly.** She wakes, transfers into her chair, moves through her morning — shown plainly, with warmth, not weight.
4. **The birthday drone.** She finds Mum's present: her **first drone**. The dream just became possible. A wobbly first flight from the rooftop = threshold crossed. (An early crash makes the stakes real and relatable.)

## Hero's-journey arc (age-appropriate, shown not told)

1. **Ordinary world — Hyderabad, home.** Family, friends, the rooftop where she watches birds and drones and *dreams*. Full personhood established before any adventure.
2. **The call — the birthday drone** (opening above).
3. **Mentor & friends.** A local pilot / repair-shop tinkerer / kind coach teaches her to fly *and to build & repair* — grounding the real FPV ladder (below) and the garage. Friends accumulate city by city; some rivals/bullies become friends.
4. **Trials — the city chapters.** Escalating tests (race / delivery / rescue / photo) themed to each city; skill and drone tier ramp together; a badge/token per city (Pokémon-gym rhythm).
5. **Setback / low point.** A real stumble — a lost race, a drone she can't afford to fix, a bully who briefly lands one, an inaccessible venue. **The obstacle is always external**; she and her friends fix the *situation* (rebuild the drone, add the ramp, master the line) — never a cure.
6. **Mastery.** She integrates skill + crew + sport chair + rebuilt drone; growth shown through *how she flies now* vs. the wobbly first flight.
7. **Championship & return — Mumbai finale.** She becomes India's best FPV racer, then comes home to Hyderabad *masterful and belonging* — mentoring the next kid on the rooftop, the world a little more open than she found it.

## City-chapter ladder (Pokémon-style)

Each city = a chapter with a distinct real flavor, a mission mix, a signature set-piece, and one earned badge. Difficulty ramps across the ladder.

| # | City | Flavor / icons | Mission fits | Signature hook |
|---|------|----------------|--------------|----------------|
| 1 | **Hyderabad** (home) | Charminar, Golconda echo, Hussain Sagar Buddha, Old City↔HITEC, biryani, Bonalu | Race, Delivery, Photo | **Charminar Chime Race** — Golconda's echo is the starting gun; old world → tech skyline in one flight |
| 2 | **Bangalore** (calm/tutorial) | Lalbagh & Cubbon greens, Vidhana Soudha, cool weather, dosa+filter coffee | Race, Photo, light Delivery | **Startup City Sprint** — thread gaps between IT towers over leafy parks |
| 3 | **Chennai** (open coast) | Marina Beach (~13 km), Kapaleeshwarar gopuram, Pongal kolams | Race, Photo, Rescue, Delivery | **Sunrise on the Marina** — race the dawn down India's longest beach |
| 4 | **Kolkata** (night precision) | Howrah Bridge, Victoria Memorial, yellow taxis & trams, Durga Puja pandals | Photo, Race, Delivery | **Pandal-Hopping at Durga Puja** — night-lights weave through thousands of pandals |
| 5 | **Ahmedabad** (the flying set-piece) | **Uttarayan kite festival**, Sabarmati, Adalaj Stepwell, dhokla | Race (kite-slalom), Rescue, Photo | **Sky Full of Kites** — weave through kite-strings *without cutting them*; rescue tangled kites |
| 6 | **Trivandrum** (water/pursuit) | Kovalam + striped lighthouse, backwaters, Onam, snake-boat races | Race, Photo, Rescue | **Race the Snake Boats** — skim palm-fringed backwaters keeping pace with a chundan vallam |
| 7 | **Delhi** (mazes + formation) | India Gate, Red Fort, Qutub Minar, Lotus Temple, Chandni Chowk, Republic Day flypast | Race, Delivery, Photo, Rescue | **Flypast Over the Capital** — join the aerial display, then a bazaar food time-trial |
| 8 | **Mumbai** (finale) | Gateway, Marine Drive "Queen's Necklace", CST, local trains, **dabbawalas**, Ganesh Chaturthi | Delivery (finale), Race, Photo | **The Dabbawala Drone Run** — join the legendary lunch relay on a strict clock; the final test |

**Difficulty ramp:** Hyderabad (home/tutorial) → Bangalore (forgiving) → Chennai (open) → Kolkata (night precision) → Ahmedabad (kite-slalom set-piece) → Trivandrum (water pursuit) → Delhi (mazes + formation) → Mumbai (timed delivery gauntlet finale).

**Cultural respect:** Bonalu, Durga Puja, Ganesh Chaturthi, Onam, and temples are living observances — celebrate their color and community; **never** make deities, idols, or worshippers obstacles or targets. Portray each city warmly and accurately; ideally have locals review each chapter.

## FPV grounding → ties into the garage & tier ladder

The real, authentic FPV on-ramp *is* our progression spine (grounds the underdog arc and the STEM/garage system):

**free/cheap simulator → tiny whoop (indoors) → local club / micro races → build & solder your own quad → open racing.**

- Maps onto the **drone tier ladder** (T0 DIY junk → T5 advanced) and the **garage** (she *builds and repairs* — "the best drones are built, not bought").
- FPV = **first-person goggles view** ("you are the drone") — a natural identity/camera mode to unlock.
- Races run through **gates and flags** (arch/keyhole/LED gates) in ~2–3-min heats — fits the mixed-mission cadence.
- Aspirational-but-honest: kids realistically climb sims → whoops → youth/club racing; frame the pro league (DRL/MultiGP) as the dream endpoint. Real young pilots (e.g., a teen sim-to-world-champion story) model the underdog path.

## Cast (starter set)

- **Ira** (default; player-named) — the hero (above).
- **Mum** — warm, believes in her; gives the first drone.
- **A mentor** — older local pilot or repair-shop tinkerer; teaches flying + building (the garage's in-world voice / guide character, per `Design-Garage-and-Learning.md`).
- **Friends** — a growing crew gathered city by city; include **disabled friends** (community, not isolation).
- **A rival → friend** — a cocky kid racer she bickers with, who becomes an ally.
- **Bullies** — beaten by skill and kindness, not cruelty; ideally redeemed.

## Open questions

- Hero name: **player-selectable, default `Ira`** (decided). Confirm age (7–9 fits the audience).
- How much explicit dialogue vs. wordless/"show" storytelling (lean wordless for the youngest; the smart-player principle favors minimal telling).
- Cutscene style (playable dream sequences vs. short animatics).
- Per-city badge/reward + how it feeds the garage economy (bolts, unlockable parts).
- Sensitivity + cultural review plan (disability consultant + city locals) before production.

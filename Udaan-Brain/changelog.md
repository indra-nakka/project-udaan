- 2026-07-06 | EnemyDroneAI.cs | **Obstacle avoidance rewrite** (enemies still got stuck). Root cause: a single forward `SphereCast` from the drone's own position can't detect a wall it's already overlapping, so once wedged it saw nothing and stayed. New system: (1) **stuck-recovery** — if commanded to move but barely moving next to solid geometry (velocity + `OverlapSphere` check), reverse out + spin + climb for 1.2s with a random turn dir; (2) **three forward whiskers** (fwd + both front-quarters) measure clearance and steer toward the more open side instead of the unreliable surface-normal; (3) **stop driving into walls** — cut throttle when close, reverse when very close. Raycast whiskers ignore other drones (only terrain blocks) | session-062
